# CS-255-DriverPass
DriverPass Project portfolio CS255 Systems analysis and Design SNHU
## Briefly summarize the DriverPass project. Who was the client? What type of system did they want you to design? 
The client was the owner, Liam. He and his IT officers met with the team to bring what they want for their system. They wanted a system that will give the options for customers to learn and schedule drving lessons to increase the number of drivers that pass the driving test. Liam wanted a three package option for the customers that included online lessons, practice tests and hands-on driving lessons with an instructor.
## What did you do particularly well? 
I did well on the UML diagrams of the system. They were concise and gave a good example on how the system would function. For example, my activity diagram showed the steps for booking a lesson and what happens when a customer does not have enough driving hours.
## If you could choose one part of your work on these documents to revise, what would you pick? How would you improve it? 
I would improve how I capture the client’s needs by getting more details, documenting their requirements clearly, and confirming that I understood them correctly.
## How did you interpret the user’s needs and implement them into your system design? Why is it so important to consider the user’s needs when designing? 
The users needs are important to see how the system will function in the way that aids them and makes it easy for them to use. Knowing what the user needs, will help build the system into a luser friendly system. The feedback that the user provides also will help create a better system for them to access and find everything that they need. I included checks for the customer’s remaining driving hours and instructor and vehicle availability before confirming a lesson. This helps customers schedule lessons without booking conflicts.
## How do you approach designing software? What techniques or strategies would you use in the future to analyze and design a system? 
The techniques i would use would be having a Scrum team and making sure there is a good line of communication between everyone to capture what the client wants in their design. Have the client be exact at what they want, get all details, make a design by formatting UML diagrams and then testing the system with the team.
